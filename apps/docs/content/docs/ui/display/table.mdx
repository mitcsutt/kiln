`Table` is a real `<table>` with Kiln's styles: rules between rows, numeric columns in tabular figures, and a wrapper that scrolls sideways on narrow screens instead of breaking the layout.

<Example name="ui/display/table/usage" />

- `Table.Cell numeric` and `Table.HeaderCell numeric` right-align figures in the theme's numeric face. In Ledger that's condensed mono, like receipt tape.
- `Table.Cell rowHeader` renders a `<th scope="row">`, the cell that names its row.
- `Table.Row highlighted` marks "you" or the row that needs attention.
- `hideBelow` on a cell drops a column on small screens. Hide the header cell and every body cell in that column.
- `Table.Foot` holds totals. Ledger rules it off with a double line.

Give every table a name: a `Table.Caption`, or `label` when the caption would repeat a heading.

## Sorting

Pass `sort` and `onSort` to a header cell and its label becomes a button with `aria-sort` set. The table doesn't sort for you: decide the next direction and reorder the rows yourself.

<Example name="ui/display/table/sorting" />

## Density, stripes and sticky headers

`density="compact"` for dense data, `striped` for alternating fills (rules usually read better), and `stickyHeader` to keep the header in view while the rows scroll inside the table.
