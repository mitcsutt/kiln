description: A measurement against a known range, whose tone follows thresholds you set, so the app never picks colours.
exports: [Meter, meterTone]

`Meter` shows where a value sits in a range: seats taken, budget spent, storage used. Give it `low`, `high` and `optimum` thresholds and the tone follows the value, so 60% of capacity is calm, 90% is a warning, and over the limit is critical.

<Example name="ui/display/meter/usage" />

`valueLabel` is the spoken and printed value ("212 of 320 seats"). `segments` splits the bar into discrete cells, for small counts.

## The tone without the bar

`meterTone` returns the tone a meter would pick. Unlike the native `<meter>`, a value past `max` still counts as past `high`: an overspent budget must not read as fine.
