`Stat` is a number that matters, with its label and how it changed. It's typographic on purpose: label, figure and change on one baseline, in the theme's numeric face. A row of them on a hairline reads better than three tiles with an icon in a tinted circle each.

<Example name="ui/display/stat/usage" />

`delta` takes a `value`, a `direction` and a `tone`. Choose the tone by whether the change is good news, not by its direction: fewer delays is `down` and `positive`. `hint` adds context such as a target, and `rule` draws a hairline above.
