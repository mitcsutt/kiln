---
title: AvatarGroup
description: An overlapping row of avatars, like who's on a crew or who reacted, with a count for the rest.
exports: [AvatarGroup]
---

`AvatarGroup` overlaps its `Avatar` children and, past `max`, replaces the rest with a count. Give it an `aria-label` that names the group.
