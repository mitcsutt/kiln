`ChoiceCardsField` is [ChoiceCards](/docs/ui/inputs/choice-cards) under a [Fieldset](/docs/ui/inputs/fieldset) legend. Use `type="single"` for one of the cards, `type="multiple"` for any number.

<Example of="ChoiceCardsField" />

Keep it to a handful of cards. Past five or six, a list is easier to scan.
