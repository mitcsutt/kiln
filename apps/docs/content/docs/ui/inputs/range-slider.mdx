exports: [RangeSlider]
---

`RangeSlider` picks a range: a departure window, a price band. The wrapper is a group, labelled by its Field or `aria-label`, and each thumb is a slider named by `thumbLabels`. `minStepsBetweenThumbs` keeps the thumbs apart.
