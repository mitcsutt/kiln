`DateRangeField` is two native date inputs, side by side and wrapping on a phone, under one legend. Native inputs mean the platform's own date picker and its accessibility.

<Example of="DateRangeField" />

`value` is `{ start, end }` as ISO date strings (`2026-10-14`). `min` and `max` bound both inputs, and the end can't be before the start. `startLabel` and `endLabel` name the two inputs.
