---
title: Quote
description: A pull quote or testimonial, with a hanging opening mark and an attribution.
exports: [Quote]
---

`Quote` sets the opening mark in its own column in the display face, the way a typographer hangs punctuation, rather than drawing a coloured bar down the side.

<Example of="Quote" />

`cite` is the attribution; `citeUrl` links it and sets the `cite` attribute on the `<blockquote>`.
