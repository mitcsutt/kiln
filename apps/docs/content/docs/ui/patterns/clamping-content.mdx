---
title: Clamping content
description: Long content gets a ShowMore around it. Layout and text components never take a clamp prop.
---

Descriptions, comments, logs and long lists often need cutting short with a way to show the rest. In Kiln that's always the same move: wrap the content in [`ShowMore`](/docs/ui/layout/show-more). `Text`, `Prose`, `List`, `TagList`, `Table` and `CodeBlock` have no `maxLines` or `clamp` prop of their own, and they won't get one.

## The rule

**Layout and text components never take clamp props. Wrap them in `ShowMore`.**

One wrapper means one toggle, in one place, with one label and one animation, whatever it wraps. It also means the clamp can't fight a component's own layout: a `Table` keeps its scrolling, a `CodeBlock` keeps its copy button, and a `TagList` keeps its wrapping.

## Pick the clamp by the content

| Content                              | Clamp       | Why                                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| A paragraph of `Text`                | `lines`     | The cut falls between lines.                             |
| `Prose`, a `Table`, a `CodeBlock`    | `maxHeight` | Their lines aren't all one height, or they have a frame. |
| A `List`, a `TagList`, a set of rows | `maxItems`  | Whole items show or hide, and the toggle counts them.    |

## Accessibility

- The toggle is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and it appears only when there's more to show.
- Text clamped by `lines` or `maxHeight` stays in the page, so screen readers read all of it. If keyboard focus lands on a link below the cut, the content opens so the focused link is on screen.
- Items hidden by `maxItems` are out of the page until the toggle shows them, and the toggle says how many there are.
- Opening and closing animate with the theme's motion tokens, and change instantly under reduced motion.
