`BottomNav` is the bar of destinations at the bottom of a phone screen. It's fixed to the bottom with room for the home indicator, and hidden from `md` up by default, where header `NavLinks` take over.

<Example name="ui/navigation/bottom-nav/usage" />

Three to five items, each with an `icon` and a short `label`. `badge` adds a dot (`true`) or a count. Here it's shown in the flow (`position="static"`, `hideAbove={false}`) so it appears in the preview; in an app, leave the defaults, or put it in `AppShell.BottomBar`, which handles the placement.
