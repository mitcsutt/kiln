Add toast notifications: mount a `Toaster` once and call `toast()` from anywhere, inside React or out. Toasts take the Alert frame and tones, one action and a close button, pause on hover and focus, can be reached with F8, dedupe by `id`, and support `toast.update`, `toast.dismiss` and `toast.promise`.
