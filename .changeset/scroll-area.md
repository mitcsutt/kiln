Add `ScrollArea`, a scroll container for content wider or taller than the page, such as a bracket or a timeline. With a `label` (or `aria-labelledby`) it's a named region in the tab order, so keyboard users can focus and scroll it; `axis` picks `x` (default), `y` or `both`, and the scrollbar takes the theme's line colour (`--scroll-area-thumb`).
