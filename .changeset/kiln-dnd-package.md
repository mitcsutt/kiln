Add `@mitcsutt/kiln-dnd`, drag and drop on kiln-ui. `Sortable` reorders a list, `Board` moves cards within and between columns, `GridLayout` moves and resizes panels on a grid with compaction and breakpoints, and `Draggable` drops onto `DropZone`s. The primitives under them are the `Dnd` scope and the `useSortable`, `useDraggable` and `useDroppable` hooks. Every pattern shares one keyboard model: Space or Enter picks up and drops, the arrow keys move, Shift and the arrows resize a grid item, and Escape cancels. Focus stays on the handle, and one live region announces each step, with English defaults you can replace through `announcements`. Mouse and pen drag once the pointer moves; touch needs a 250ms press. Nothing changes until the drop: lists show a drop line, grids a placeholder, and `onChange` fires once with plain data. Lists are arrays, boards are `{ column: [...] }` maps and grid layouts are `[{ i, x, y, w, h }]`, with `arrayMove`, `moveBetween`, `appendItem`, `removeItem` and `compact` to change them in code. The drag engine (dnd-kit's DOM core) loads in the browser only, so server rendering never touches it, and `@mitcsutt/kiln-dnd/testing` stubs what jsdom lacks. Import `@mitcsutt/kiln-dnd/styles.css` after kiln-ui's stylesheet.
