Riso: one brush-lettered family, status as extra ink drums, and a printed blue page.

- Riso is now set in Recursive at its Casual master for every role except code, replacing Shantell Sans. Body copy stays handmade but reads easily, and its digits are tabular, so columns of dates, counts and money align. Headings are 800, table heads 700. The theme ships four instanced Recursive files (upright and italic, latin and latin-ext, about 46–55 KB each), and the Shantell files are gone.
- Status chips are heavier flat screens of each extra drum instead of pastel pills, and critical leans orange-red so it no longer passes for the pink accent tint. Riso sets `--badge-soft-ink` to `currentColor`, so soft badges print in the surrounding ink: blue (or night) on the page, and the band's own ink inside inverse, accent or categorical bands.
- The light page is a 12% screen of the blue drum, with matte off-white sheets a step up, raised layers one more step and sunken wells a pink-drum tint. No light fill goes above L 0.97, and Riso no longer shares a light canvas with Ledger.
