The `design-rules` agent skill now gives each built-in theme its goal, the kind of product to choose it for: Paper for products that shouldn't have a personality yet, Ledger for numbers people have to trust, Flightdeck for operating something live, and Riso for products made for people. It also notes that Monograph and Fiesta are deprecated and leave in the next minor release ([ADR 0045](https://github.com/mitcsutt/kiln/blob/main/docs/adr/0045-four-themes-each-with-a-goal.md)). No code changes.
