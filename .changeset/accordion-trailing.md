`Accordion.Trigger` takes `trailing`, a figure or badge set at the end of the trigger just before the chevron, and its label now fills the trigger, so content inside it can spread to the far edge. With `trailing`, a small trigger's label fills too, so a list of small rows lines its figures up on the end edge; a small trigger without one still hugs its text.
